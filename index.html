About Me

AI and backend engineering, from prototypes to production systems.

I'm an AI Engineer and B.S. Computer Science graduate of Information Technology University (ITU) Punjab (2026), focused on building reliable AI and backend systems for production use.

I currently work at DevOrbis on generative AI and RAG solutions. Previously, I trained and evaluated LLMs as a contractor for Turing, interned at Zenvyro Labs, and built CLARA, a RAG backend, along with a geospatial routing API as a Software Engineer Intern at Neuralige.

My work treats AI as backend infrastructure: retrieval pipelines, LLM orchestration, agent workflows with human-in-the-loop controls, and well-designed REST APIs backed by PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and vector stores, containerized with Docker.
