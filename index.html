About Me

The short version: I design the systems that move data — and people — efficiently.

I'm a final-year B.S. Computer Science student at the Information Technology University Punjab, graduating in 2026, with hands-on experience shipping backend systems in production engineering teams.

Most recently at Neuralige, I architected the Fuel Route Optimizer, a Django REST Framework API that integrates OpenRouteService and Geopy to compute multi-stop routes and minimize travel cost — the kind of problem where geospatial logic, caching strategy, and clean API design all have to work together.

Alongside routing systems, I build AI-native backends: RAG pipelines with LangChain and ChromaDB, real-time web search retrieval with Tavily, and LLM orchestration through OpenRouter — treating AI features as backend infrastructure, not a bolt-on.

Earlier, at AppifyTech, I worked across FastAPI, Django, and Flask, shipping JWT-secured endpoints and ML preprocessing pipelines with NumPy, Pandas, and Scikit-learn.
